**System Name:** SkillPulse LMIS  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

Modern labour markets face accelerated transformations driven by automation, emerging technologies, green transitions, and evolving industrial priorities. Across national and state ecosystems, a critical challenge persists: **the temporal and spatial mismatch between skill supply produced by training institutions and dynamic skill demand generated by industry**.

**SkillPulse LMIS** is designed as an AI-enabled Labour Market Information System (LMIS) that aggregates, maps, analyzes, and forecasts skill demand-supply dynamics at regional, state, and national levels. The engine provides policymakers, State Skill Development Missions (SSDMs), training providers (ITIs, polytechnics, universities), and industry stakeholders with actionable foresight to align curricula, allocate training seats, and mitigate emerging skill shortages before they stifle economic mobility.

---

## 2. Core Principles & Boundary Conditions

To maintain analytical integrity and real-world viability, the platform operates under strict engineering principles:
1. **Taxonomy-First Normalization:** Ground all job roles and qualifications into established national and international occupational standards (such as NCO 2015, NSQF, and ESCO).
2. **Explainable Forecasting:** Avoid opaque "black-box" outputs; provide confidence intervals, explicit driver weights, and historical ground-truth benchmarks.
3. **Strict Data Hygiene:** No unauthorized website scraping, no synthetic or hallucinated government statistics, and zero reliance on inaccessible private commercial APIs. All ingested datasets are driven by public, standardized, or official administrative open-data schemas.
4. **Modularity & Scalability:** High-performance, loosely coupled backend architecture built on FastAPI, enabling independent evolution of analytics, forecasting, and simulation components.

### Module 1: Data Ingestion Pipeline
- **Purpose:** Ingest, sanitize, and validate structured feeds representing market demand signals, educational throughput, and demographic workforce indicators.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Automated schema validation using Pydantic models.
  - Multi-source batch ingestion (CSV, JSON, open government datasets).
  - Data cleansing pipelines: deduplication, missing-field imputation, and boundary verification.
  - Audit logging and lineage tracking for every ingested batch.

### Module 2: Occupation & Skill Mapping Engine
- **Purpose:** Harmonize fragmented job titles and disparate qualification names into standardized occupational frameworks.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Mapping to National Classification of Occupations (NCO 2015) 4-digit and 8-digit codes.
  - National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF) level attribution.
  - Semantic clustering of job role descriptions against standard skill ontologies (e.g., technical competencies, soft skills, foundational tools).
  - Resolution of colloquial or evolving industry titles into standardized occupational clusters.

### Module 3: Demand Index Engine
- **Purpose:** Quantify relative market appetite and hiring momentum across sectors, states, and districts over time.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Synthesis of multiple normalized demand indicators (posting volume, replacement demand, emerging industry announcements).
  - Spatial aggregation: District-level and State-level Labour Demand Indices (LDI).
  - Sectoral weighting to prevent over-representation of high-frequency white-collar postings relative to critical vocational trades.

### Module 4: Training-Supply Estimation Engine
- **Purpose:** Model the actual and upcoming pipeline of certified talent across educational and skilling channels.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Aggregation of sanctioned vs. enrolled vs. graduated seat capacity across ITIs, Polytechnics, PMKVY centres, and higher education.
  - Attrition modeling: accounting for historical dropout rates, certification pass percentages, and inter-state migration factors.
  - Multi-cohort graduation timelines mapping when students will actively enter the job market.

### Module 5: Gap Forecasting Engine
- **Purpose:** Compare projected occupational demand against the upcoming training supply pipeline across multiple temporal horizons.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Multi-horizon forecasting: Short-term (3 months), Medium-term (6 months), and Extended (12 months).
  - Quantitative Gap Metric:
    $$\text{Projected Relative Mismatch} = \text{Forecast Demand Postings Rank} - \text{Forecast Certified Throughput Rank}$$
    - *Metric Distinction:* Forward projections evaluate single-quantity posting ranks against throughput ranks, differing from Module 3's weighted multi-signal Demand Index used in current-period Gap Analysis.
  - Structural vs. Cyclical classification: differentiating between persistent pipeline shortages and short-term hiring surges.
  - Confidence interval estimation: RMSE normal error approximations expressing uncertainty transparently.

### Module 6: Early-Warning Alerts & Anomaly Detection
- **Purpose:** Proactively flag acute skill deficits, saturated trades, and emerging technological disruptions before they manifest as critical bottlenecks.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Threshold-triggered alert rules (e.g., demand outstripping supply capacity by >2.5x over consecutive quarters).
  - Saturation warning system: identifying declining trades where thousands of students are graduating into stagnant or disappearing roles.
  - Anomaly detection flagging abnormal shifts in hiring requirements or localized skill surges.
  - Multi-tiered alert levels (Informational, Caution, Critical Shortage).

### Module 7: Seat-Allocation & Policy Simulator
- **Purpose:** Provide an interactive "what-if" decision support sandbox for administrators and training bodies to optimize seat allocation.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Proportional capacity scaling: adjusting future sanctioned cohort seats and scaling certified graduate throughput under an empirical constant-yield pipeline assumption.
  - Recalculated rank mismatch: recomputing cross-sectional regional supply percentile ranks against the comparison population for the selected horizon.
  - Percentile-rank saturation: modeling rank ceiling effects when proposed expansions surpass regional cross-sectional throughput benchmarks.
  - Explainable policy impact: comparing baseline vs. scenario throughput and mismatch deltas side-by-side with prototype heuristic threshold disclosures (&plusmn;15 points).

### Module 8: Interactive Decision Dashboard (Planned Frontend)
- **Purpose:** Deliver clear, responsive visual analytics tailored to key user personas.
- **Target Personas & Views:**
  - **Policymakers / SSDMs:** National and state geospatial heatmaps, supply-demand gap overview, budget allocation prioritization.
  - **Training Institutes (ITIs / Colleges):** Course rationalization guidance, local district industry demand trends, placement alignment.
  - **Industry Bodies / Employers:** Regional talent pipeline visibility, upcoming graduate cohorts by specialization.
  - **Youth & Career Counselors:** High-demand skill trajectories, career path recommendations, local training center discovery.

### Module 9: Interoperable API & Open Data Export
- **Purpose:** Ensure seamless integration with existing government digital infrastructure and external stakeholder platforms.
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - RESTful API with automated OpenAPI / Swagger documentation.
  - Fine-grained query parameters (filtering by state, district, sector, NCO code, horizon).
  - Export utilities supporting open data formats: JSON, CSV, GeoJSON (for geospatial analysis), and automated PDF policy briefs.

## 5. Non-Functional Specifications

- **Latency:** Core API query responses under 150 ms for cached regional indices.
- **Modularity:** Stateless API layer enabling effortless horizontal scaling.
- **Security:** Strict input sanitization, rate-limiting, and environmental secret separation via `.env`.
- **Reproducibility:** Versioned dependencies, automated setup scripts, and platform independence across Windows and Linux environments.
